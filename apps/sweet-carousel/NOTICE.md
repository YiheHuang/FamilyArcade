# NOTICE · Sweet Carousel / 甜点转盘

Sweet Carousel is an **original, non-commercial family card game** made for a home game-night collection. It is not an official product of any board-game publisher.

## Originality

- **Mechanics are inspired by public pick-and-pass / card-drafting conventions.** Mechanics are not copyrightable; the expression here is original.
- **No third-party card text, artwork, board layout, logo or publisher name is used.** Every dessert name, card face, icon, colour, pattern and line of copy in this repository was written or drawn for this project.
- **All illustrations are code-drawn SVG shapes** in `src/ui/components.tsx` and the CSS. There are no external art assets, fonts, textures or audio files.
- The game does not use the name, characters, card text or visual identity of any published game. Any resemblance is limited to the general idea of choosing a card and passing the rest.

## Privacy

No accounts, no telemetry, no network requests at runtime. Settings, stats and the current game live in the browser's `localStorage`. The exported JSON contains only the player names, local scores and timestamps you can see in the app.

## Family safety

No gambling, violence, ads, loot boxes, daily streaks or purchases. The game is designed for ages 7+ and for a shared family device.
