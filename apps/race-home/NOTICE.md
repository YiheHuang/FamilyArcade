# NOTICE · Race Home / 飞行棋

Race Home is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. The
Ludo-like “race around the ring and bring four pawns home” mechanic is a traditional
public-domain folk game. This implementation, its name, board composition, copy,
visual design and source code are original work for Family Arcade.

- No third-party art, icons, font files, music or audio samples are included.
- The board, pawns, house, dice and lane markings are drawn in CSS and inline SVG.
- Sound effects are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API; there are no
  sample files.
- No remote fonts, CDNs or tracking scripts are loaded.
- Code and original assets are MIT licensed. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- “Ludo” and “飞行棋” are used only to describe a traditional game family; any
  trademarks mentioned in general discussion belong to their respective owners.
