# NOTICE · Chess / 国际象棋

Chess is a public-domain classic. This Family Arcade implementation — its engine,
copy, layout, CSS and original assets — is made for the family table.

- No third-party art, textures, font files, audio samples or remote assets are
  included.
- The board, highlights and promotion dialog are drawn in CSS. Piece glyphs are
  Unicode chess characters rendered by the system font; they are not a bundled
  font file.
- The app icon is an inline SVG in `index.html`, drawn for this project.
- Sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- Narration for the root promo is synthetic Edge TTS; music is synthesised from
  scratch by the root video studio.
- Code, copy and original assets: MIT. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Chess rules and standard algebraic notation are public knowledge. Trademarks
  mentioned in general discussion belong to their respective owners.
