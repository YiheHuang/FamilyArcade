# NOTICE · Domino Line / 多米诺

Domino Line is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. The
double-six block-domino mechanic is a public-domain folk game; this implementation,
its name, copy, visual design and code are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- Every domino tile, pip, tile back, table surface and icon is drawn in CSS/SVG for
  this project.
- QR codes are generated locally with the MIT-licensed `qrcode` npm package. No QR
  image is downloaded.
- The promo narration is rendered by the root Family Arcade studio from `video.json`;
  this app contains no third-party music or samples.
