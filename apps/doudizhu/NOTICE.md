# NOTICE · Dou Dizhu / 斗地主

Dou Dizhu is a public-domain folk card game. This Family Arcade implementation,
including its engine, names, copy, CSS card art, table design and synthesised
sound, is original work made for this project.

- No third-party card art, photographs, font files, music or audio samples are
  included.
- All cards, backs, table surfaces, marks and icons are drawn with CSS/SVG in this
  repository.
- Sound effects are synthesised with the browser Web Audio API. Narration for the
  promo is synthetic Edge TTS.
- The shared LAN relay is the repository’s `family-arcade-lan-host` crate; the
  browser relay is `tools/lan-server.mjs`. Both are part of Family Arcade.
