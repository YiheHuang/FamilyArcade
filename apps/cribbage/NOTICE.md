# NOTICE · Cribbage / 克里比奇

Cribbage is a public-domain traditional card game. This implementation, its name, copy,
visual design and code are original Family Arcade work.

- Two-player six-card and three/four-player cut-throat five-card rules.
- No third-party art, card scans, font files or audio samples are included.
- Every card face, card back, peg board and icon is drawn with CSS or inline SVG.
- System font stacks only; no remote fonts or CDNs.
- The promo, when rendered by the root studio, uses synthesised audio and synthetic
  narration from the project pipeline.
