# Blokus v1.5.0

**Nobody has to read the manual — and nobody is dropped in the deep end.** A short illustrated guide greets first-time players, and hints now explain themselves.

## What's new

- **First-run guide** (also reachable from the home page): three little pictures show how to drag, turn and confirm, followed by the rules in five lines and the complete 21-piece set. Shown once, skippable, always one tap away.
- **Hints that explain themselves**: tapping Hint highlights a placement and adds a plain-words reason — "keeps your expansion routes open", "hugs the edge — harder to surround", "blocks an opponent while it expands" or "biggest piece — grab the space".
- Small polish everywhere: the e2e suite now covers the guide, the hint reason and every social feature end to end.

This completes the second round: **sound & feedback (v1.1) → phone-first play (v1.2) → replay & stats (v1.3) → undo votes, pause, nudge, spectators (v1.4) → onboarding & explanations (v1.5)**.

## Install

Download `Blokus_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it — per-user install, no admin required. Your settings, stats and replays stay.

The release page shows the SHA‑256 digest of the uploaded installer under *Assets*.
