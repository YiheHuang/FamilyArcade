# Blokus v1.7.1

One illustration fix, nothing else changed.

## Fixed

- **“How to play” guide, step 3 (“Click to confirm”)**: the S-piece was drawn one cell too far to the right and stuck out of its 3×3 grid. It now sits fully inside the patch, with the cursor and the green check in place.

Everything from v1.7.0 is unchanged: the lobby QR code, 4-digit room codes, the AI caretaker, “continue last game”, the LAN deep scan and the social round of undo votes, pause and spectators.

## Install

Download `Blokus_1.7.1_x64-setup.exe` below and run it — per-user install, no admin required. Your settings, stats and unfinished game stay.

The release page shows the SHA‑256 digest of the uploaded installer under *Assets*.
