# Blokus v1.1.0

**Sound, and knowing what just happened.** Family game night rarely means everyone staring at the screen — so the board now tells you.

## What's new

- **Zero-asset sound effects** (synthesized live, no downloads): a soft tap when a piece lands, a two-note chime when it becomes your turn, a low blip for an illegal placement, an arpeggio at the end of the game — and a little fanfare when someone clears all 21 pieces.
- **See every move:** when another player (or the AI) places a piece, its squares are outlined for a moment with their name right above it. Perfect for when someone looked away — or was raiding the kitchen.
- **"+15 clears!" celebration:** finishing all 21 pieces flashes the board frame and announces the bonus.
- **Sound controls:** `M` cycles on / low / off during a game; the home screen has a persistent toggle. Your choice is remembered.

## Install

Download `Blokus_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it — per-user install, no admin required. Already on v1.0.0? Just run this installer; your settings stay.

The release page shows the SHA‑256 digest of the uploaded installer under *Assets*.
