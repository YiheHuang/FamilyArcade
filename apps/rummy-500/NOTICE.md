# NOTICE · Rummy 500 / 五百分拉米

Rummy 500 is an original, non-commercial family implementation of a traditional
public-domain card game. This implementation, its name, rules text, visual design,
sound and code are original to Family Arcade.

- One standard 52-card deck; no jokers, no wild cards.
- No third-party card art, rulebook text, font files, music or audio samples are
  included. Cards, table and icons are drawn in CSS/SVG.
