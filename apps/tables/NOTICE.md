# NOTICE · Tables / 双陆棋

Tables is an original, non-commercial family implementation of backgammon for Family Arcade.
Backgammon is a public-domain game; this implementation, its name, copy, visual design
and code are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- The 24-point board, triangles, checkers, dice and icons are drawn in CSS.
- Music and sound in root-studio promos are synthesised from scratch; narration is
  synthetic Edge TTS. This app itself ships no recorded audio.
