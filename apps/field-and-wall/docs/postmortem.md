# Postmortem — Field & Wall v0.1

**What went well**

- The engine was built and tested before the UI, so city/road/monastery scoring, meeple
  majority/return and final scoring were stable before the first screen.
- A curated 42-tile demo game, generated by the engine, made screenshots and the video
  show a real mid-game instead of an empty board.
- The AI Worker/public-view boundary was kept from the start; the bag order never reaches
  the AI.
- The root-studio manifest (`video.json`) carries real screenshots, demo scores and eight
  bilingual lines; the previous-pass cuts were mastered to −14 LUFS with voiceover.

**What to fix in v0.2**

1. AI calibration: the 200-game table is done, but Normal vs Hard is still within noise;
   tune Hard before claiming an ordering.
2. LAN polish: spectate, pause, undo vote and host migration are the next modes.
3. Add screenshot pixel baselines and branch coverage to CI.
4. Validate the central Tauri installer on a clean Windows machine.
5. Playtest with two families; tune the 30–35 minute target and the 54-tile mix.
