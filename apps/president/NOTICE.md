# NOTICE · President / 排位赛

President is an original implementation of the public-domain climbing card game also
known as Scum or Capitalism. The rules are folk rules; this implementation, its name,
copy, visual design and code are original.

- No third-party art, card faces, font files or audio samples are included.
- Every card, table, badge and icon is drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- Music and sound in the promo are synthesised from scratch; narration is synthetic
