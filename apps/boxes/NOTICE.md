# NOTICE · Boxes / 点格棋

Boxes is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. Dots and boxes
is a public-domain pencil game; this implementation, its name, copy, visual design
and code are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- All dots, lines, boxes and marks are drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- Sound is synthesised from scratch with the Web Audio API.
- The promo is rendered by the root studio from `video.json`; narration there is
  synthetic TTS, produced by the shared pipeline rather than this app.
