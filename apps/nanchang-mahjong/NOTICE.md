# NOTICE · Nanchang Mahjong / 南昌麻将

Nanchang Mahjong is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade.

- Mahjong is a traditional public-domain tile game. The 136-tile set, the names of the
  winds and dragons, and the Nanchang family rules are traditional. This implementation,
  its code, its balance choices, its copy and its visual design are original.
- No third-party art, tile scans, fonts, music or audio samples are included.
- All tiles, table surfaces, icons and the favicon are drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- Sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API; there are no audio
  files in the app.
- The QR code on the LAN screen is generated at runtime by the MIT-licensed `qrcode`
  package. The LAN relay and client helpers are the shared `@family-arcade/lan` package.
- Runtime libraries are React, React DOM, Vite and the shared Family Arcade LAN package,
  all under their respective MIT licenses.

  their respective owners.
