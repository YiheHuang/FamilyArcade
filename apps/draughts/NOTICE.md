# NOTICE · Draughts / 国际跳棋

Draughts is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. English
draughts is a public-domain folk game; this implementation, its name, copy,
visual design and code are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- The board, pieces, crowns and icons are drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- Sounds are synthesised from scratch with the Web Audio API.
- The 45-second promo is rendered by the root Family Arcade studio from `video.json`;
  narration is synthetic TTS at build time.
