# NOTICE · Little Go / 小围棋

Little Go is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. Go is a
traditional public-domain board game; this implementation, its name, copy, visual
design, code and the demonstration positions are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files, audio samples or downloaded images are
  included.
- The board, stones, marks and icons are drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- Sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- The 45-second promo is rendered from `video.json` by the root Family Arcade studio; no
  third-party footage is used.
