# NOTICE · Zheng Shangyou / 争上游

Zheng Shangyou is an original, non-commercial family implementation of a
traditional public-domain Chinese climbing card game. The name, rules summary,
code, UI copy, layout, card rendering, sound and visual design in this app are
original to Family Arcade.

- No third-party art, card faces, font files, music or audio samples are included.
- Cards, felt, chips and icons are drawn with CSS and inline SVG for this project.
- The promo is rendered centrally from this app's `video.json`; narration is
  synthetic and music is synthesised from scratch by the root studio.
- The game uses a standard 52-card deck and no jokers.
