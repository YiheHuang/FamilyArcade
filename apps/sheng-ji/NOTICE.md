# NOTICE · Sheng Ji / 升级

Sheng Ji is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. The
traditional four-player partnership trick-taking rules are public-domain folk
knowledge; this implementation, its code, copy, visual design, card faces, table,
icons and sound are original to this project.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- All card faces, felt, marks, buttons, the felt table and the app icon are drawn in
  CSS/SVG for this project.
- The QR invite is generated locally from the `qrcode` package; no external image
  service is contacted.
- Sound effects are synthesised with the Web Audio API at runtime.
- The 45-second promo is rendered centrally from [`video.json`](video.json); this app
  stores no Remotion project or media asset.
- Code and original assets: MIT. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Trademarks and game names mentioned in general discussion belong to their
  respective owners. This app is not affiliated with any publisher or tournament
  organisation.
