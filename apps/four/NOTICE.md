# NOTICE · Four / 四子棋

Four is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade. The
four-in-a-row mechanic is a public-domain folk game; this implementation, its
name, copy, visual design, code and screenshots are original.

- No third-party art, card text, font files or audio samples are included.
- The board, discs, icons and favicon are drawn in CSS/SVG for this project.
- The only audio is a short oscillator chime generated at runtime.
- Screenshots in `docs/screenshots/{en,zh}` are captured from the running app
  with Playwright; no mockups are used.
- The promo is described by `video.json` and rendered by the root Family Arcade
  studio; this app does not ship a separate Remotion project.
