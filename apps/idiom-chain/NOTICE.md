# NOTICE · Idiom Chain / 成语接龙

Idiom Chain is an original, non-commercial family game made for Family Arcade.
The chain-of-idioms mechanic is a public-domain folk game; this implementation,
its name, copy, visual design, code and bundled dictionary are original.

- The 680 idioms are traditional Chinese phrases in the public domain. Their
  selection, frequency labels, pinyin, short English glosses and dictionary
  order were written for this project. No third-party corpus, commercial
  dictionary, word list or audio sample is included.
- No third-party art, font files, images, music or remote assets are included.
  The paper textures, seals, chain marks and layout are drawn in CSS/SVG.
- Sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API.
- The app runs offline. LAN play uses the family's own Wi-Fi and the shared
  Family Arcade relay; there is no cloud service.
- Code and original assets: MIT. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Trademarks mentioned in general discussion belong to their respective owners.

The Chinese text that appears in the English interface is game content (idioms,
pinyin and the language switch), not untranslated interface prose.
